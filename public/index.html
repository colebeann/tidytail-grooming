Our promises

What we will and won't do

No sedation, ever

If your pet is too stressed, we stop, tell you, and try again another day at no charge.

Real reviews only

We never buy, fake, filter or delete reviews. We only ask real customers to share an honest one.

Your pet's records are yours

Ask for your photos, notes and history any time, and we'll send them. Ask us to delete them and we will.

Price agreed first

You see the price after the free check and before we start. If we find something new, we call you first.

One groomer, one pet

No cages full of dogs waiting. Your pet has one groomer from start to finish.

Stop messages any time

Say "stop" on LINE or use our opt-out page. We stop at once and won't message you again.

Questions

Is the free check really free?

Yes. It takes about 15 minutes and you don't have to book anything after it. You keep the report card either way.

Do you need vaccination records?

Yes, please bring a rabies and core vaccine record at the first visit. We keep a copy only with your permission.

What if my pet bites or panics?

We take breaks, use muzzles only if you agree, and stop if it isn't safe. We never sedate. You only pay for what we finished.

How do I pay?

Cash, LINE Pay or card on the day. Care Club is prepaid at the start of each month and you can stop any time.

What do you do with my details?

We use them only to arrange your pet's visit. They are encrypted on our server, never sold, and deleted after 12 months without a visit. See our privacy notice.
