Who we are

Tidytail Grooming Studio (sample business), No. 00, Sample Road, Dajia District, Taichung. Contact: LINE @tidytail-demo, privacy@tidytail.example.

What we collect

  • Your name, mobile number, LINE ID and/or email.
  • Your pet's name, type, breed, size, coat or health notes you choose to share.
  • The service, area and date you ask for.
  • Basic technical logs (time, page, response code). We do not log form contents, and we use no advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your request and arrange your pet's visit.
  • Only if you ticked the optional box: grooming reminders and seasonal offers on LINE.

We never sell your details or share them for other companies' marketing.

How we protect it

  • The site only works over HTTPS.
  • Your details are encrypted on our server (AES-256-GCM) and only the owner and staff who handle bookings can read them.
  • Phone numbers and emails on our opt-out list are stored as one-way keyed hashes.
  • Requests without a visit are deleted after 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, get a copy of, or delete your details, and to stop all marketing at any time. Message us on LINE or email privacy@tidytail.example. To stop marketing messages straight away, use the opt-out page or reply "stop" on LINE.

Cookies

We set one strictly necessary security cookie to protect the booking form from forged requests. It expires after 2 hours. We remember your language choice in your browser only.
